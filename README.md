# Initial Design and Planning Process
Day 2: Bascially what I want this proj to be: A vertical garden made to fit in an apartment, which will water plants, track moisture in the soil, and surrounding temperatures. Thinking of adding a feature to check nutrient density in the soil as well. This proj will also have a nearby screen which logs the plants in the garden and lists the info about the plant in question.

The finalised system is that there'll be a lower reservoir which just sits next to the vertical garden and then an archimedes screw (automated using a motor) to transport the water upwards and then a drip irrigation system which distributes water to each plant. (This is the current plan, I might end up using some sort of sprinkler system later).
This design is for the water system, but I'll also be adding EC sensors and NPK fertilisers to each pot to track the moisture, nutrient density, pH, and humidity. There will also be a camera just for tracking the growth over time, and to make the proj really useful we'll be adding a dedicated PC connected to everything to log data.

I also want to add sensors to check the water levels in all 3 reservoirs and the reservoir temperature because ill be using clay to make them, inspired by bindi, traditional indian water coolers basically- also cs clay is very durable esp in summer.

Day 3: The water system is the most important to the whole project and therefore i want it to be as efficient as possible.
Im using an archimedes screw to bring water from the lower reservoir (just a small tank with bunch of water) to the upper reservoir (another longer tank with water). From here, there will be rails leading downwards and a lot of openings which open using controls on an external PC. Its a little complicated, but, the PC will also be able to read the mositure on each level, and then being able to decide which level needs the most water and then it'll water said level and re-check moisture levels- building a self-sustaining system.

I'll need to see if this is physically possible and if the effort is worth the result first though.
